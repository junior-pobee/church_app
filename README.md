# Kingdom Connect — Digital Church Platform

> Your Spiritual Home. Faith. Fellowship. Service. Community.

Kingdom Connect is a modern church management and discipleship platform I built to help churches connect with members beyond Sunday service.


Built by: Emmanuel Pobee Okpoti (@junior-pobee) | Founder of Adapt OS | Accra, Ghana


### Why I Built This

Most church apps are just for streaming. I wanted to build a full platform that handles real church life — from Bible study and prayer requests to giving, events, and admin. A place where members can actually grow.

### Features

**For Members:**
- **Dashboard** - Personal spiritual overview
- **Discover & Media** - Sermons, worship, and church media library
- **Community** - Connect with fellow members
- **Prayer & Pastoral Care** - Submit prayer requests and get pastoral support
- **Bible & Growth** - Daily Scripture for Reflection (e.g., Psalm 23:1), Bible Gateway & YouVersion integration, Personal Reflection Journal
- **Events & Programmes** - Church calendar and registrations
- **Giving Centre** - Secure tithes and offerings

**For Admins:**
- **Admin Centre** - Manage members, content, and church operations
- **Branding & Settings** - Customize the platform for any church

### Tech Stack

- Bible API: Bible Gateway, YouVersion
- Storage: Local browser storage for private reflections

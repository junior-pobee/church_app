## ✦ WELCOME TO KINGDOM CONNECT ✦

**Your Spiritual Home. Faith. Fellowship. Service. Community.**

</div>

Kingdom Connect is a modern church management and discipleship platform built to help churches connect with their members beyond Sunday service.

It brings together spiritual growth, church communication, prayer support, media, events, giving, and administration in one digital environment.

The vision is simple: **make church life more connected, accessible, organized, and engaging.**

### Why I Built This

Many church applications focus primarily on livestreaming or displaying church information.

Kingdom Connect takes a broader approach.

The goal is to create a platform that supports the wider experience of church life, from Bible study and prayer requests to events, giving, community engagement, and administration.

It is designed around the belief that digital tools can support meaningful relationships without replacing the importance of physical fellowship, pastoral care, or personal faith.

> **The mission:** Use technology to help churches stay connected, organized, and spiritually engaged throughout the week.

### Core Technology

**Frontend:** HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

**Bible resources:** Bible Gateway and YouVersion links or integrations, depending on the configured implementation.

**Local storage:** Browser-based storage for supported local data, including personal reflections where implemented.

### Architecture Principles

- Responsive user interfaces.
- Clear separation between member and administrative experiences.
- Organized church content and resources.
- Appropriate handling of personal information.
- Secure server-side processing for sensitive operations.
- Integration with external services only where configured.

**Important:** Browser local storage is not a shared database. If members on different devices must see the same announcements, updates, records, or events, the application needs a shared backend and database.

Similarly, payment processing and sensitive church financial records should use secure services rather than relying solely on browser storage.
